Scrawlbound Support

Scrawlbound is a hand-drawn adventure on iPhone and iPad. Draw what the page needs, and it comes alive.

Common questions

  • Restore a purchase. Open the page that offers the whole book and choose restore purchase while signed in to the Apple Account used for the purchase.
  • It read my drawing wrong. Tap the hero's guess to choose what you meant. Simple outlines with the main features, such as legs, wings or wheels, read best.
  • Bring in a picture. Choose a drawing or photo from Photos or Files. A clear picture of one main thing works best.
  • Drawing pauses the page. That is on purpose. Take your time.

Privacy and data

Scrawlbound works offline and your drawings stay on your device. Read the full Privacy Policy.
