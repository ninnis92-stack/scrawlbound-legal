Scrawlbound Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026

Scrawlbound is a hand-drawn adventure game by Naheem Innis. This policy explains what Scrawlbound does with information.

In short: Scrawlbound works fully offline. It has no account, no advertising and no analytics, and the developer does not run a server. Your drawings and progress stay on your device, and sync only through your own private iCloud when you are signed in.

Your drawings and progress

Your drawings, saved progress and settings are stored on your device. Scrawlbound does not upload them to the developer, and the developer cannot see them. If you are signed in to iCloud, your heroes, their progress, your kept drawings and your settings also sync through your own private iCloud account, so they follow you across your own devices and survive reinstalling the game. That data lives in your private iCloud storage, which only you (and Apple, as your iCloud provider) can access. You can stop it with the icloud tick in the game's settings, by turning off iCloud for Scrawlbound in your device's Settings, or by signing out of iCloud. If you use iCloud Backup for your device, Scrawlbound's data is also part of that backup, which Apple manages.

Reading your drawings

When you draw something, Scrawlbound works out what it is using models built into the app. This happens entirely on your device. Your drawings are never sent anywhere to be recognised.

Pictures you import

You can choose a drawing or photo from Photos or Files to bring into the game. Scrawlbound only sees the picture you choose. It finds the main subject and turns it into a sketch on your device, using Apple's Vision framework. Pictures are not sent to the developer.

Purchases

The purchase that unlocks the whole book is handled by Apple through the App Store. Scrawlbound learns whether you own it so it can unlock the pages. The developer does not receive your payment details. Apple handles billing and refunds.

Beta testing

Test versions through TestFlight include a tester's page that can keep a drawing log on your device: what you drew, what the game read it as, what you chose and how long each page took. It never leaves your device unless you choose to export it and send it yourself. This log is not part of the App Store version. Apple may share crash reports and feedback you choose to send through TestFlight, under Apple's TestFlight terms.

Tracking, advertising and analytics

Scrawlbound does not track you, show advertising, or include analytics or other third-party SDKs. It does not collect data types that need to be disclosed in the App Store's privacy labels.

Deleting your data

Deleting Scrawlbound from your device removes its saved progress and drawings.

Children

Scrawlbound does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions, email n.k.innis92@gmail.com. You can also open an issue in the public Scrawlbound Legal GitHub repository. Please do not send passwords, payment information or other sensitive information.
